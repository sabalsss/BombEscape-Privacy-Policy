Who we are

Tap Arrows: Bomb Escape (package com.sabalapps.taparrowsbomb) is an offline puzzle game for Android published by [Publisher name – pending confirmation] (“we”, “us”).

The short version

  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal information.
  • The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app does not request internet access; your progress stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the app saves a small amount of information locally on your device using Android’s app storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Game progress – unlocked levels, stars and best times.
  • Settings – sound, haptics, theme and reduced-motion preferences.
  • Tutorial state – which tutorial steps you have completed or skipped.
  • Reminder preferences – whether daily reminders are on, your chosen reminder time, and the date of the last reminder.
  • Invitation preferences – whether you chose to rate, share, be asked later or not be asked again, and how many levels you have completed since.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not used to identify you.

Notifications and daily reminders

Daily reminders are off by default. If you turn them on in Settings, the game asks for Android’s notification permission (Android 13 and later) and schedules one local reminder a day at about the time you choose, using Android’s on-device scheduler (WorkManager). Reminders are created on your device; no server is involved and no data is sent anywhere. You can turn reminders off in the game’s Settings or in Android’s notification settings at any time.

Third-party services

  • Google Play In-App Review – if you choose to rate the game, the rating flow or the Play Store listing is provided by Google Play. Anything you submit there is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. The game is never told whether or how you rated it.
  • Android Sharesheet – if you choose to share the game, Android shows its own share menu and the app you pick sends a short message you can edit. We do not see who you share with.
  • Android backup – if you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the game’s saved progress and settings in your device backup, under your Google account and Google’s policies. This is controlled by your device settings.

The game contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social-login SDKs.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) – only to show the optional daily reminder.
  • Android’s scheduler may also use standard system permissions it needs to run scheduled work reliably (for example, to restore schedules after a restart). They are not used to collect data.

Data retention and deletion

Everything the game saves stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings (Apps → Tap Arrows: Bomb Escape → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the game. Because we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children’s privacy

The game is suitable for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If the game’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
